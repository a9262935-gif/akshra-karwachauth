CHAPTER 03 • THE SECRET DATES

Oberoi Cafe Diaries

12 July ki wo tareekh dil par hamesha ke liye chhap chuki thi, jab pehli baar humne ek-doosre ko gale lagaya aur wo pehla ehsaas jiya tha... Par uske baad jab bhi milne ka plan banta, kisi na kisi wajah se ruk jata tha. Hazaron koshishon aur lambe intezaar ke baad aakhirkaar Oberoi Cafe wali wo pehli official date ka din aaya! ✨

Aap apni tuition se sidha aayi thin aur main pure shauq se tayyar hokar aapko Mandi lene pohncha tha. Dil mein bas ek hi zid thi—is din ko aapke liye zindagi ka sabse anmol din banana hai. Main apne ghar se khuloos ke saath ek mehakta hua laal gulab lekar aaya tha. Jab wo rose maine aapko diya, hum dono ka yun sharma jaana, chupke se nazrein milana aur wo dher saari photos khichwana... us pal cafe ka poora mahaul humare pyaar se mehak utha tha. 🌹🙈

Cafe ke baad jab hum bazaar nikle, toh aapki pasand ki wo choti-choti cheezein—wo pyare clippers, clutches, hair accessories chunna aur dhoondh-dhoondh kar aapke liye Dairy Milk chocolate lena... CHAPTER 04 • THE FAREWELL VOWS 🚗🌧️

Baarish, Gaadi Aur Wo Pehla Sparsh

8 February 2026 ko humari 1st Anniversary par door rehne ki kami ko 10 February ki Farewell ne hamesha ke liye mita diya. Sirf aapke sath wo anmol waqt bitane ke liye pehli baar bina kisi ko bataye, darr aur dhadkanon ke beech gaadi ki chaabi uthayi. Jam mein faskar aur har mushkil paar karke jab main school pohncha, toh Head Boy ki speech aur poora function ek taraf tha, aur dil mein sirf aapka intezaar doosri taraf... 🌧️✨

Function ke baad jab rimjhim baarish shuru hui, toh aakhri dance chhod kar main apna coat sambhalte hue bhagta hua aapke paas aaya—'Rukiye, main gaadi leke aata hoon!' Pehle aapka peeche baithna, meri choti si narazgi par muskura kar aage aana, aur gaadi chalate waqt steering ke paas mera haath thaam lena... us lamhe ne saari thakan aur darr ko sukoon mein badal diya. 🚗❤️

Gaadi ke andar ka wo silsila toh dil par taaza hai. Jab maine ghutne par baith kar apne haathon se aapko wo naye shoes pehnaye—taaki meri bachi ke paanv hamesha safe aur khush rahein—aapki aankhon ki wo khushi anmol thi. Apne haathon se banaya humara Youth Parliament wala sketch, dher saari KitKat aur chocolates, aur hamare ghar ke mandir jaisi pavitra Shiv-Parvati ki moorti... jo yeh gawaahi deti hai ki humara yeh roohani bandhan janam-janam ka hai. 🌸🍫🕉️

Aur wo pyari si meethi nok-jhok... pehle inkaar, gaalon par kiss, aur fir achanak nazrein milte hi wo halka sa, anmol pehla lip touch. Thodi si sharm, thodi si zid aur behisaab pyaar se bhari wo shaam meri zindagi ka sabse haseen sach ban gayi. 🙈💋
